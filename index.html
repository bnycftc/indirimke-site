An autonomous studio for short-form video.

Shorts Creator Pro takes a video from idea to published Short in one click: topic discovery, research, script, visuals, voice, render, quality checks and scheduling — with Claude doing the thinking at every step.

How it works

01
Topic discovery

Finds evergreen and trending topics per channel niche and filters out anything already covered.

02
Research & script

Gathers sources, checks facts and writes a tight script with a hook, structure and closing question.

03
Visuals & voice

Plans scenes, pulls licensed stock or generated footage, records narration and animates a talking mascot presenter with lip-sync.

04
Render & QA

Renders vertical video, then runs automated quality, caption and originality gates. Anything that fails goes to human review.

05
Publish

Schedules approved videos to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels with titles, descriptions and a first comment.

Today

  • Two languages. Running English and Turkish channels from the same pipeline.
  • Built on Claude. Topic selection, fact checking, scriptwriting and QA judgments are made with Claude.
  • Quality first. Strict gates for originality, factual accuracy and duplicate prevention before anything goes live.
  • Bootstrapped. Shorts Creator Pro is built and operated independently by Indirimke, a solo-founder company.
